what worked — key strategic decisions
✓
China local plant (approved Y3, operational Y4). Single biggest financial lever of the simulation. F&T dropped from CNY 619M to CNY 30M. Gross margin went from 6.1% → 25.2% in China. Generated ~$400M+ additional cumulative contribution vs home plant scenario.
Plant cost $555.8M but paid back in Year 4 alone through margin improvement
✓
Whitening pivot (started Y3 Korea, Y4 China/India/Philippines). Every Whitening SKU beat forecast by 2-4x. Korea Whitening/Md/Gel hit 35.6% GM. Philippines Whitening/Sm hit 28.7% GM. China Whitening hit 35-37% GM — best margins in portfolio.
Entering Whitening 1-2 years earlier would have been even better
✓
India new creative (Y5). Creative Exec jumped from 3 → 66 with culturally appropriate Hindi creative. India went from BEI 20 → 63 peak, 7.1% → 28.1% market share. Became your #2 market by revenue.
The Y2 ad score of 3 was the most expensive marketing mistake of the simulation
✓
South Korea restructuring (Y3). Cut ad budget KRW 4,998M → 500M, added Wholesale, pivoted to Whitening. Turned -KRW 4,256M loss into profitable market in one year. Maintained profitability every year since.
BEI 63 in final year — highest of all markets in Y6
✓
Philippines: Market leader. 31.5% share, market leader throughout Y5-Y6. Creative execution was consistently excellent (95-100 scores). Whitening franchise dominant at 65%+ of segment.
!
What we'd do differently: Enter Whitening earlier. Didn't launch Whitening in China until Y4, India until Y4. Both markets showed 2-4x demand vs forecast. Two extra years of Whitening = ~$200M more cumulative contribution.
Also: Philippines Healthy/Lg (3.3% GM in Y5) should have been dropped a year earlier
!
Japan — left on the table. $3.6B market, 0% tariff, never entered. Entering in Y4 would have added ~$150-200M cumulative contribution over 3 years. Risk-aversion cost us.

Year 7 — Final year. Close the BEI gap. Protect the profit lead.
We trail on BEI by 7 points (67 vs 74) and market share by 1.9pp. We lead by $477M on cumulative contribution. Year 7 strategy: aggressive brand building to close BEI, targeted SKU additions to improve benefit position, protect margins at all costs. Do not chase their sales volume — their ROMI of 9.2% proves they are buying share unprofitably.
year 7 priority actions — ranked by BEI impact
1
China: Add 3rd benefit message — Kids/Families campaign. Currently running Whitening/Younger + Healthy/Families. Adding a Kids campaign covers a third benefit segment and directly lifts Benefit Position BEI from 60. Budget CNY 80M on the new Kids ad.
Three simultaneous benefit campaigns = fastest single BEI lever available
2
Philippines: Add one Healthy SKU back. Benefit Position collapsed to 23 after dropping Healthy/Lg. Add Healthy/Sm/Tube/Pst at PHP 55-60 — even small volume signals benefit breadth to the BEI algorithm. This is the fastest way to recover Benefit Position in Philippines.
You don't need to sell much — just need the SKU present to register benefit coverage
3
India: Increase advertising to INR 800M+. Creative Exec dropped to 37 from war impact — more spend compensates. Push Share of Mind (35) toward 45+. Add a Whitening/Younger campaign to support Whitening/Lg.
Also consider new creative — Y7 is last chance to recover India BEI from 56
4
South Korea: Increase advertising to KRW 4,000M. BEI 63, Creative Exec 88 — this is your healthiest market. More spend here translates directly to Share of Mind improvement (currently 47). Push toward 55+.
S.Korea is your most efficient BEI market — every KRW spent here has the highest BEI return
5
China: Add Independent reps (30 → 40). Sales Leadership is 37 — still your biggest BEI gap in China. Independent is 54.5% of the market and you have 8,223 outlets covered but more reps = more facings, better placement, better sales leadership score.
Each Independent rep added lifts Sales Leadership proportionally
6
India: Add Whitening/Lg/Tube/Gel if not already in. 6.2M units in Y6 debut. Scale with stronger forecast. Benefit Position needs breadth — large Whitening adds premium tier coverage.
Caremore leads at 33.6% largely through Whitening/Pump — counter with Whitening/Lg
7
Review allowances — trim 1-2pp where possible. India allowance 18.3%, China 21.5% — both above optimal. Even 1pp reduction in India = INR 418M saved on INR 41B sales. This flows straight to contribution and widens the profit gap further.
Do not reduce allowance on new SKUs — only on established high-volume SKUs with strong demand

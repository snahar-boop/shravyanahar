Project · Simulation

Allsmile Asia

GTM Asia Simulation · Summer 2026 · Team of three

Turning market data into a growth strategy

A seven-year CountryManager business simulation exploring how to grow an oral-care brand across China, India, South Korea, and the Philippines while balancing profitability, market share, and brand equity.

My role

I personally designed and built the dashboard to support our team’s decisions. I brought regional performance, country financials, SKU margins, distribution, and competitive benchmarks into one interface, making it easier to identify problems and prioritize our next moves.

From analysis to action

The dashboard helped frame decisions about local manufacturing, product expansion, advertising, and distribution. Its Year 6 review connected performance gaps to a Year 7 plan: protect profitability while strengthening brand equity through targeted market investments.

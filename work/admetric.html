Can AdMetric Ethically Increase Their Profit?

Ethics for Managers · Santa Clara University · Fall 2025 · Team of six

We applied an ethics framework to an ad-tech case, and I built the algorithmic section that defined where persuasion becomes manipulation.

~2×

What was the question?

In the case, behavioral nudges lifted conversion from 2% to 3.5% but nearly doubled 90-day churn. Is that profit ethical?

What did I own?
  • The algorithmic section. Using AI tools, I charted how conversion, churn and returns move against LTV/CAC.
  • A numeric threshold for when persuasion becomes manipulation.
  • A tolerance band that switches a feature off once it crosses set limits.

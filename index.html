About

I started in bioengineering — medical devices, disposables, validation — and kept gravitating to the questions upstream of the drawing: who is this for, what will it cost, and why now. That pull took me into product management, and now into an MBA at Santa Clara University's Leavey School of Business.

I'm optimizing for roles where the business case and the mission point the same direction — sustainability, medicine, hardware that has to actually work. Almost a decade of launching physical products has made me stubborn about customer evidence and comfortable with messy trade-offs.

Raised in Chennai, seasoned in the UK, now building in the Bay Area — a useful habit of assuming the customer doesn't live where I do.

Curious, analytical and thoughtfully optimistic.

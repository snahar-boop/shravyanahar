Where do the questions come from?

I started in bioengineering — medical devices, disposables, validation — and kept gravitating to the questions upstream of the drawing: who is this for, what will it cost, and why now. That pull took me into product management, and now into an MBA at Santa Clara University's Leavey School of Business.

I’m optimizing for roles where the business case and the mission point the same direction — sustainability, medicine, hardware that has to actually work. Almost a decade of launching physical products has made me stubborn about customer evidence and comfortable with messy trade-offs.

Raised in Chennai, seasoned in the UK, now building in the Bay Area — a useful habit of assuming the customer doesn't live where I do.

Curious, analytical and thoughtfully optimistic.

What did I find when I dug in?
Go-to-Market Strategy for a Life-Sciences Reagents Manufacturer MBA practicum, Spring 2026. With a team of six, built a three-phase plan for a US reagents maker to enter Europe. I led the market analysis (103 target accounts across seven countries), competitor benchmarking against 14 rivals, and pricing intelligence, and owned the distributor and local-manufacturing phases. We found EU distributors marked up one product by about 270%, and that consolidated pallet shipping could cut per-unit freight by roughly 90%. Go-to-Market Capeltic Go-to-Market Strategy Marketing course, Fall 2025. With a team of five, built a go-to-market plan to bring an Indigenous-owned coffee cooperative into Jesuit universities and parishes. I owned three parts: the five key marketing challenges, the three buyer segments (campus food-service directors, student clubs, faculty and staff), and the packaging and menu story. Go-to-Market GTM Asia Simulation Summer 2026. Seven-year market-entry simulation for a consumer-health brand expanding across Asia. Our team of three led the three competing teams on cumulative contribution ($924M), finishing with 17.8% market share and a brand equity score of 77. Using AI tools, I built the weekly dashboard that turned 18 data sheets into results and next-step recommendations. Simulation Can AdMetric Ethically Increase Their Profit? Ethics for Managers, Fall 2025. With a team of six, applied an ethics framework to an ad-tech case where nudges lifted conversion from 2% to 3.5% but nearly doubled 90-day churn. I built the algorithmic section: using AI tools, charted how conversion, churn and returns move against LTV/CAC, then set a numeric threshold for when persuasion becomes manipulation and a tolerance band that switches a feature off once it crosses set limits. Case Study Predicting the 2026 World Cup Ranked national teams to predict the 2026 World Cup winner from a pre-tournament dataset. The model's ranking matched teams' pre-tournament points exactly, a useful reminder to test any model against the simple baseline before trusting it. Analytics
